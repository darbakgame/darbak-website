Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what data the DARBAK UAE game for iPhone processes, why, who we share it with, and how to delete your account.

1. Scope and responsible publisher

Who we are

This policy explains data processing in the “DARBAK | دربك” game, its website and support service, based on current features. The responsible publisher is Humaid Al Derei (HUMAID ALDEREI), an individual publisher in the United Arab Emirates. Contact: darbakgame@gmail.com.

We get this data in three ways: what you enter when you create an account, what is recorded automatically while you play, buy or watch ads, and what the game saves on your device.

We do not collect your precise location (GPS), contacts or photos, and the game has no chat or private messages. The advertising SDK may process crash and diagnostic data as described in the ads section. Apple may also share crash reports according to your device settings.

What other players see

The Top 10 leaderboard shows everyone your account name and score, and may show your in-game plate details, so choose a name that does not reveal your identity.

3. Website and support

Website and support communications

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process connection and usage data, such as your IP address, browser and device information, and request time, under the GitHub Privacy Statement, to provide, protect and operate its services. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which requires your browser to connect to Google’s servers.

When you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send so we can handle your request and respond. Send only necessary information, such as your game version, device type and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment card details. You may ask about or request deletion of your support data using the same email address.

External links are governed by their operators’ policies. Game and game-service data differ from website visits and support communications.

5. Storage, providers and data protection

Service providers

We use providers to run accounts, data, payments and ads, and we share data only with providers that give it protection equal to this policy. We do not sell your data, and we do not disclose it to anyone else unless the law requires it.

  • PlayFab (Microsoft): accounts, sign-in and password recovery. Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: game data, scores, missions, and verifying purchases and rewards. Privacy Policy
  • Apple: in-app purchases (StoreKit). Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and UMP: rewarded ads and consent requests. Privacy Policy

The in-game credits screen links to external websites, each with its own privacy policy.

Processing outside your country

Your data may be stored or processed by our providers in countries other than yours. [PlayFab and Supabase server regions — to be set by the owner]

Ads and consent

DARBAK uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads when available and enabled for the game version and advertising service. Players choose to watch an ad for the offered benefit, and rewards are granted after server-side verification.

The advertising service may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, available device or app identifiers, device, performance and crash data, and ad impressions and interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Reward verification sends Google verification data including a user identifier and reward-request data. The game service records display, closure and reward events.

The game uses Google UMP to update consent information and display privacy messages when required. It requests ads only when UMP permits requests. You can review your choices using the in-game “Ad privacy options” button when available. Ad eligibility and type depend on consent, configuration and region. The absence of a consent prompt in your region does not mean no data is processed.

The current iOS integration does not directly request ATT tracking permission. This does not mean the advertising service processes no other identifiers or technical data. See Google’s advertising policy and Google’s Privacy Policy. Opening an ad or visiting an advertiser’s site is also subject to the receiving party’s policy.

Data security

Data travels between the game and our servers over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we take reasonable measures to protect it, but no system is completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Account deletion and retention

  • How to request deletion: Delete account page.
  • The in-game and backend deletion procedure for PlayFab and Supabase must be technically verified before publication.
  • Purchase records (transaction ID, product and time) are kept without any link to you, to prevent duplicate purchases.
  • Ad event retention periods are pending verification against provider and server configuration.
  • Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored progress. Device and backup behavior should be verified.

The actual account-deletion processing period remains to be verified against the backend and provider procedures. A copy may remain in providers’ backups until their retention period ends, after which it is deleted automatically; we do not use these copies for any other purpose.

7. Your rights

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information about processing, access or a copy of your data, correction, deletion, portability, objection or restriction, and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on it. Send your request to darbakgame@gmail.com or use the Support page. We may request proportionate information to verify your connection to the data and handle requests under applicable legal deadlines and requirements. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority in your country.

8. Children and policy updates

Children

DARBAK is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes

When we change this policy we update the “Last updated” date above, and we notify you in the game if the change is significant.

Beta testing

This website currently has no beta registration form or application-data collection form. If you email darbakgame@gmail.com to ask about testing DARBAK, we process your email address and message as support communications described in this policy. Do not send Apple or Google passwords or verification codes.

If beta registration becomes available, the registration notice will explain the required data, its purposes, recipients, retention period and eligibility conditions before submission. Contacting support alone does not mean you have been accepted for testing or subscribed to marketing messages.

DARBAK news mailing list

The website currently has no newsletter subscription form or integration with Kit or a similar mailing-list service. We do not automatically add people who contact support to a marketing list.

If a news mailing list becomes available, the registration notice will identify the provider, data used, purposes and unsubscribe method, and request any necessary consent. For questions about your existing support correspondence or to request its deletion, contact darbakgame@gmail.com.
