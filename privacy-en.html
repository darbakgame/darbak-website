Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what data the DARBAK UAE game for iPhone processes, why, who we share it with, and how to delete your account.

1. Scope and responsible publisher

Who we are

This policy explains data processing in the “DARBAK | دربك” game, its website and support service, based on current features. The party responsible for processing personal data is “DARBAK | دربك”, based in the United Arab Emirates. Contact: darbakgame@gmail.com.

Website and support communications

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. 4. Processing purposes

Why we process it

  • Providing the game you asked for: your account, online play, rewards, the Top 10 leaderboard, and saving your progress and settings on your device.
  • Completing your purchases: verifying the purchase and granting coins.
  • Your consent: rewarded ads; you can withdraw consent at any time in the game.
  • Protecting the game: preventing cheating and manipulation of scores and rewards.
  • Understanding players’ broad geographic distribution: deriving the connection country and recording its code with the account for analytics, without identifying your precise location.

5. Storage, providers and data protection

Service providers

We use providers to run accounts, data, payments and ads, and we share data only with providers that give it protection equal to this policy. We do not sell your data, and we do not disclose it to anyone else unless the law requires it.

  • PlayFab (Microsoft): accounts, sign-in and password recovery. Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: game data, scores, missions, and verifying purchases and rewards. Privacy Policy
  • Apple: in-app purchases (StoreKit). Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and UMP: rewarded ads and consent requests. Privacy Policy

The in-game credits screen links to external websites, each with its own privacy policy.

Processing outside your country

Your data may be stored or processed by our providers in countries other than yours, depending on the infrastructure locations they use. This processing is subject to the safeguards described in this policy and the provider policies linked above.

Ads and consent

DARBAK uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads when available and enabled for the game version and advertising service. Players choose to watch an ad for the offered benefit, and rewards are granted after server-side verification.

The advertising service may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, available device or app identifiers, device, performance and crash data, and ad impressions and interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Reward verification sends Google verification data including a user identifier and reward-request data. The game service records display, closure and reward events.

The game uses Google UMP to update consent information and display privacy messages when required. It requests ads only when UMP permits requests. You can review your choices using the in-game “Ad privacy options” button when available. Ad eligibility and type depend on consent, configuration and region. The absence of a consent prompt in your region does not mean no data is processed.

We do not use device identifiers to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, or share them with data brokers for that purpose. The current iOS integration does not directly request ATT tracking permission. This does not mean the advertising service processes no other identifiers or technical data. See Google’s advertising policy and Google’s Privacy Policy. Opening an ad or visiting an advertiser’s site is also subject to the receiving party’s policy.

Data security

Data travels between the game and our servers over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we take reasonable measures to protect it, but no system is completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Account deletion and retention

Request deletion: Sign in, then open Settings → Account → Delete account, re-enter your password and select “Delete my account permanently”. Your password is used for verification within the game’s deletion flow; never email it. If you cannot sign in or receive an error, email darbakgame@gmail.com from your account email with your account name so we can verify ownership and follow up. See the account deletion page.

Deletion scope: A deletion request is sent to PlayFab, followed by removal of the account and linked game records from the game database, including progress, balances, assets, scores and account-linked advertising records. On a successful response, the saved sign-in session is removed from the device and the player leaves the cloud account. This does not erase separate local-player files or device backups.

Retained records: Purchase transaction records, including transaction ID, product, environment and recording time, are retained with their link to the account identifier removed, to prevent the same transaction being credited more than once. The current flow sets no automatic expiry for these records. Deleting the game account does not delete records independently managed by Apple or Google.

Event and advertising logs: The current retention mechanism targets usage events and ad-verification records that did not grant a reward once they are older than 90 days, when cleanup runs. This is not a uniform retention period for all data. Verification records that granted a reward are excluded from this time-based cleanup; account-linked records are removed through the account deletion flow.

Completion time: PlayFab processes deletion in stages. A successful game response does not mean every provider system or backup has been erased at that instant. See PlayFab’s deletion explanation. We do not specify a single retention period for providers’ backups and operational logs. Contact us about your request’s status or remaining data. Uninstalling the app or signing out alone does not request cloud-account deletion, and deletion does not automatically refund purchases.

8. Children and policy updates

Children and age suitability

DARBAK is a driving and exploration game for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. Check the age rating shown on the game’s App Store page in your country before downloading. This policy does not set a numerical rating in place of the store’s rating or a legal age for consent to data processing.

The game does not request a date of birth or include age verification or a verified parental-consent flow. We therefore cannot determine that an account user or ad viewer is an adult merely because they use the game. The absence of an age question does not mean accounts or advertising process no data; see the data and advertising sections.

The current iOS advertising integration requests a maximum ad content rating of PG (parental guidance). This limits ad content; it is not the game’s age rating or a check of the player’s age, and does not automatically enable child-directed ad treatment or disable personalization for every user. UMP manages privacy choices as described in the advertising section.

Parents and guardians should review account features, leaderboards, advertising and purchases, and use device and store parental controls as appropriate. Choose a player name that does not reveal a child’s identity and avoid sending unnecessary child data to support. If you believe your child has provided personal data or their account needs review or deletion, contact darbakgame@gmail.com. We review the request and verify the connection to the account as necessary under the deletion and rights procedures in this policy. Do not send passwords or identity documents unless necessary information is requested through an appropriate method.
