Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what data the DARBAK UAE game for iPhone processes, why, who we share it with, and how to delete your account.

1. Who we are

“DARBAK | دربك” is a driving game for exploring cities and regions across the United Arab Emirates, featuring driving challenges, racing and vehicle customization. It is owned and operated by Humaid Al Derei (HUMAID ALDEREI), an individual publisher based in the United Arab Emirates who is responsible for processing users’ personal data.

This policy covers the DARBAK iPhone app, this website and support communications. For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact darbakgame@gmail.com.

We get this data in three ways: what you enter when you create an account, what is recorded automatically while you play, buy or watch ads, and what the game saves on your device.

We do not collect your precise location (GPS), contacts or photos, and the game has no chat or private messages. We do not use a third-party crash reporting tool; Apple may share crash reports with us only if you allow it in your iPhone settings.

Website and support communications

External links are governed by their operators’ policies. Game and game-service data differ from website visits and support communications.

4. What other players see

The Top 10 leaderboard shows everyone your account name and score, and may show your in-game plate details, so choose a name that does not reveal your identity.

5. Service providers

We use providers to run accounts, data, payments and ads, and we share data only with providers that give it protection equal to this policy. We do not sell your data, and we do not disclose it to anyone else unless the law requires it.

  • PlayFab (Microsoft): accounts, sign-in and password recovery. Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: game data, scores, missions, and verifying purchases and rewards. Privacy Policy
  • Apple: in-app purchases (StoreKit). Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and UMP: rewarded ads and consent requests. Privacy Policy

The in-game credits screen links to external websites, each with its own privacy policy.

8. Account deletion and retention

  • How to request deletion: Delete account page.
  • The in-game and backend deletion procedure for PlayFab and Supabase must be technically verified before publication.
  • Purchase records (transaction ID, product and time) are kept without any link to you, to prevent duplicate purchases.
  • Ad event retention periods are pending verification against provider and server configuration.
  • Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored progress. Device and backup behavior should be verified.

The actual account-deletion processing period remains to be verified against the backend and provider procedures. A copy may remain in providers’ backups until their retention period ends, after which it is deleted automatically; we do not use these copies for any other purpose.

9. Data security

Data travels between the game and our servers over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we take reasonable measures to protect it, but no system is completely secure.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information about processing, access or a copy of your data, correction, deletion, portability, objection or restriction, and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on it. Send your request to darbakgame@gmail.com or use the Support page. We may request proportionate information to verify your connection to the data and handle requests under applicable legal deadlines and requirements. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority in your country.

11. Children

DARBAK is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children.

12. Changes

When we change this policy we update the “Last updated” date above, and we notify you in the game if the change is significant.
