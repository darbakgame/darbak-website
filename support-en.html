FAQ

Do I need an account to play?

No, you can play as a guest. The Top 10 leaderboard and syncing coins and progress need an account and an internet connection.

I moved to a new device. Where are my coins?

Purchased coins are linked to your account. Install the game and sign in with the same account.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, choose password recovery and you will receive an email at your registered address.

I saw an offensive name in the Top 10

Email us the name with a screenshot, and we will review it and take appropriate action.

How do I request a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.
