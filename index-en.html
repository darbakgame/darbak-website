Game release status

Ways to play

Open road trips and driving challenges in one game.

Free road trip

Choose a route on the UAE map and drive between cities, with your last reached city saved.

Moreeb Hill: the sand challenge

Drive through the Liwa dunes and try hill climbs, freestyle driving and drifting. Keep your car in good condition and complete challenges within the round’s time limit.

Your choice matters

Every car has strengths and weaknesses in top speed, acceleration, stability, handling and fuel economy. Pick the right one for the challenge and change its colour.

Yas Circuit

Test your car in races or free laps and work on improving your lap time.

Daily and weekly missions

Complete daily missions and weekly emirate challenges to earn in-game rewards.

Top 10

Compare scores on leaderboards and in weekly competitions when signed in and connected to the internet.

Your plate, your style

Own your favourite plates from across the emirates and fit them to your car.

Road hazards

Growing traffic, speed radars, fog, sandstorms, broken-down cars, sudden swerves and crashes ahead. Collect fuel and boost cans to keep going.

One-handed play

Play in portrait: one stick to accelerate, brake and steer, and a drift button.
