Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about accounts, progress, purchases and support.

Can I play without an account?

Yes. Guest mode offers local play without an account. Online competitions, account synchronization, and purchase and reward verification require an account and internet connection.

Can I restore my progress?

Cloud progress depends on successful sign-in and synchronization. Local guest progress may be lost when app data is removed.

How do I delete an account?

In the game, open Settings → Account → Delete account. If you cannot sign in, follow the instructions on the account-deletion page.

What game modes are available?

DARBAK includes intercity trips, Yas Circuit and Moreeb Hill challenges. Some account-based rounds and competitions require a server connection.

How do I contact support?

Email darbakgame@gmail.com.
