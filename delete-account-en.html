Delete your account and data

You can delete your DARBAK account and its game data at any time, in either of two ways.

1. In the game

  1. Open DARBAK and sign in
  2. If available in your game version, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  3. Follow the verification method shown in-game; never email your password

2. Without opening the game

Send the request from the email address registered to your account, and include your account name.

Send deletion request

What is deleted, what is kept, and how long deletion takes are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Important: Emailing a deletion request does not mean it is immediately completed. Ownership is verified and completion is confirmed under the actual deletion procedure.
